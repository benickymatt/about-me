About Me

With five years of industry experience in technical and professional writing, I am dedicated to producing original content that is accessible to a variety of audiences. Approaching a new journey in my career, I aim to advance my skill set and knowledge of the field by working towards achieving a doctoral degree in Technical Communication and Rhetoric.

Expertise

Technical Writing Team Leadership

Technical Editing

Understanding team strengths, bandwidth, and overall project feasibility was critical in a position where documents were required with little turnaround. Establishing the Advisory Team as the last line before information is disseminated came with the power to realize what should be shared and what avenues were there to ensure communications fit the correct users. My leadership experience taught me the value in rejecting requests and staying in line with the capabilities of the team. Now as an instructor, this industry experience has provided me with experiences that I utilize in my teachings to prepare students for what they will experience in their respective fields.

Clear, accurate, and accessible documentation are all achieved only when diligent editing is conducted. My industry and academic experiences have prepared me for a wide variety of editing situations and materials. The act of technical editing has shown me how to collaborate with subject-matter experts to ensure their words are being represented on the page and that a user will be able to equally act on said words.

Visual Communication Design

Content Management Systems Operations

Graphic design originally started out as a hobby I used to crop a friend onto random images. Now, I have turned this hobby into one of my most proud skillsets that has helped me apply document design strategies onto instructional and advisory documentation. Multimodal communication is now a primary component of my work where I actively think through ways to take the words I put on a document into a visual argument. Each claim, each position, and each set of data present an opportunity for me to apply these intentional design techniques to the indented audience.

Content Management Systems have become an integral part of modern industry publications. Throughout my career, I have become an expert in programs such as Service Now, Sudden Impact, and Jira to ensure documentation was created following organizational requirements and met accessibility standards to ensure any user would be able to use our instructional materials. I had even worked with Jira developers to create and manage our content page through workflows, migrations between team content, and user performance monitoring.
